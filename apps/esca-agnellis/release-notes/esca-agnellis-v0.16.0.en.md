# Esca Agnellis v0.16.0

Esca Agnellis v0.16.0 is the first open-source release.

## What's new

- added an optional companion with small rewards and interactions;
- added a separate backup for companion data;
- added PDF reports created directly on the device;
- improved language, theme, and general usability;
- made the first-launch and information pages clearer;
- published the app source under its open-source licence;
- kept the app fully local, with no Internet permission, accounts, ads, analytics, telemetry, cloud sync, or background data collection.

## Downloads and updates

Use the official download links on the [Esca Agnellis project page](https://kamui2040.github.io/K2040-Android-Releases/apps/esca-agnellis/).

GitHub, APKPure, and Uptodown provide the K2040-published app. F-Droid builds and signs its own version from the public source, so switching between F-Droid and a K2040-published APK may require reinstalling the app. Create a backup before switching.

## Privacy

Tracking and optional companion data stay on the device. Backups and PDF reports are created only when you choose a file or location through Android.
